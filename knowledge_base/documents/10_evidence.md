Dividend per share and the capital-structure weight are inherited derived inputs.
The archive does not reconstruct their raw source filings. Do not call these

## Is every résumé method fully supported without qualification?

The code supports CAPM/factor, WACC, Gordon, DCF/NPV, option model and Greeks
calculations and a chronological ML experiment. Scope still matters. The original
Asian example is a call-price comparison, not a matched receipt-protection test.
Ridge was originally a forecast comparator, not an allocated strategy. Original
mean-variance weights were clipped rather than a true constrained optimisation.
ADR calibration is not independent market-price validation. Explain these exact
boundaries instead of asserting that every broad interpretation is implemented.

## Can I describe improvements without changing the submitted claim?

Yes, when they really exist and are clearly labelled as later work. Explain the
original method and observed result first, acknowledge its assumptions, and then
describe the specific later correction and its changed result if asked. A later
implementation does not retroactively prove an earlier claim. Do not tune data,
delete losing periods or change scores merely to make a résumé number look better.
